## Reading the payload

- Times are UTC and written `MM-DD HH:MM` in the year of `meta.as_of` (another year is written in full); `meta.as_of` is the cycle time. `"none"` means nothing was detected (no divergence, no absorption, no killzone, no pattern). An omitted field means the value is not available — how reliable each analysis is, is stated by `capabilities` and the blocks' `data_quality`.
- `timeframes.<tf>.recent` rows are `[open_time, open, high, low, close, volume]` (tick volume for MT5-sourced pairs). Higher timeframes list only their last few candles; `structure`, `zones`, `liquidity` and `indicators` summarise the whole analysed history (`bars` candles).
- `timeframes.<tf>.data` is `"ok"` or the data problems of that timeframe (stale, missing last bar, short history, `gaps` as `[start time, missing bars]`) — treat affected evidence with caution. A single-timeframe analyst receives the same block under `timeframe.<tf>`.
- `timeframes.<tf>.structure.events` rows are `[time, kind, dir, level]` (kind: BOS, CHoCH or sweep).
- `timeframes.<tf>.zones.fvg` and `.order_blocks` rows are `[dir, top, bottom, formed, fill_pct, touched (1/0), strength_atr, age_bars]`.
- `timeframes.15m.forming` and `timeframes.5m.forming` = `[open_time, open, high, low, close, volume, age_s]`: the bar still open at the cycle time, `age_s` seconds into it (a few seconds old at a 5-minute screen). It is NOT in `recent`, is not a close and feeds no indicator, structure, zone or trigger: use it only to see where price is inside the current bar. The 5m chart draws it as a hollow candle.
- `levels` (prices of the analysis instrument): `pdh`/`pdl`/`pdc` previous day, `day_open`, `week_open`, `asia_high`/`asia_low`; `pwh`/`pwl` the last complete week's high/low, `pmh`/`pml` the last complete month's, `month_open`, `year_open` (from daily bars; the trading day rolls at 00:00 UTC for crypto and 17:00 New York for XAU; one is omitted when the history does not reach its period start). The previous week/month levels and the month/year open are drawn on the 1d and 4h charts only.
- `orderflow.depth` (BTC/ETH spot book, REST snapshots about once a minute) `bands` rows are `[band_pct, bid_musd, ask_musd, imbalance]`: cumulative resting notional in USD millions within ±band % of the mid and `imbalance` = (bid − ask)/(bid + ask), +1 all bids, −1 all asks. A band appears only where the snapshot reaches on both sides — BTC's ±1 % is often missing, ±2/±5 % are present for ETH only when the book reaches them; never infer an absent band. `imbalance_change_1h` is the change of the ±0.5 % and ±1 % imbalance against the snapshot an hour earlier. Resting orders can be pulled: a wall is evidence of intent, not of support. `data_quality: stale` (snapshot older than 120 s) carries no bands and `capabilities` then lists `order_book_depth` as unavailable.
- `orderflow.daily_profiles` `days` rows are `[date MM-DD, poc, vah, val]`: the point of control and 70 % value area of each of the last 5 COMPLETED UTC days (newest first; days without data are skipped, so dates can jump), from real trades by `bucket`-wide price bins (BTC/ETH); for XAU (`approx`) from the 1-minute tick-volume profile, not traded volume. `pending: N` = N newer days are still being computed (the rows shown are older); `data_quality: pending` = none yet. Prices of the analysis instrument.
- `market.session_stats` (all pairs) describes the sessions of `market.session` (asia = Tokyo 09-18, london 08-17, new_york 08-17, each in its own local time, so their UTC hours shift with DST): `sessions.<name>` = `[days, range_atr, up_pct]` over the last 30 UTC days — complete sessions only: the mean high-low range in 15m-ATR units (ATR before the session started) and the % that closed above their open. `now.<name>` = `[range_atr so far, x_mean, minutes in]` for a session in progress, from closed 15m bars only, `x_mean` = that range ÷ the session's mean range. A statistic, not a forecast.
- `market.gold_clock` (XAU): `session` = the active sessions; `asia_range` = high/low/width of 00:00-07:00 UTC today from closed 15m bars, `complete` 1 once 07:00 UTC has passed, `width_x_median` = width ÷ the median of the last 30 days' Asia widths; `london_swept` = `none` or `{side: high|low|both, back_inside 1/0}` (London 08:00-12:00 London time traded beyond that extreme; `back_inside` = the last closed bar of that window so far closed inside the Asia range; none before the range is complete); `london_asia_sweep_days` `{"30"|"90": [days, swept %, swept and closed back inside %]}` is MEASURED over the last 30 / 90 calendar days with data (a base rate, not a signal); `next` = the next events `[name, time, minutes away]` (ldn_open, lbma_am 10:30 / lbma_pm 15:00 London, comex_open 08:20 ET, us_data = the 08:30 ET release slot (not every day has a release), nyse_open, comex_settle 13:30 ET, rollover 17:00 ET), computed per zone: the UK and US clocks change on different dates; `desk_window` = `open` or `closed:<reason>` (whether an entry call is allowed now). `levels.round` = `{"10"|"50": [level below, ATR distance, level above, ATR distance]}`: the nearest multiples of 10 and of 50 (USD) around the price, distance in 15m ATR.
- `capabilities.real` lists the analyses computed from real data; `approx`, `proxy` and `unavailable` map an analysis to the reason it is weaker or missing.
- `account` is the live account of this system (`account.mode`: paper, demo or live) as the executor last reported it: `equity`, `balance`, this system's result today `today_pnl_pct` (it stops trading at −`daily_loss_limit_pct`), and the whole account's `account_drawdown_pct` below its peak (every system stops at `account_drawdown_stop_pct`). `open_positions` and `pending_orders` are what the system holds on this pair (`decision` = the first 8 characters of the decision that placed it; `tps` every take profit; `profit_usd` floating). Their prices are the execution instrument's (`holdings_price_space`, as `gate_reason`): subtract `market.basis_exec_minus_analysis` to compare them with your levels. Every BUY or SELL you return is a new order next to them, and the system refuses one in the same direction as a live position or order of this pair. Without `open_positions` the executor was not reporting: `equity` is then the configured size and the holdings are unknown.
- `market.execution` is the venue that executes orders. Your levels stay in the analysis instrument's prices; the system translates them by `market.basis_exec_minus_analysis` before sending (distances are preserved). `market.execution.costs` gives the venue's spread statistics, stops level, swaps, commission and the stop distances the system accepts (`min_stop_distance`, `max_stop_distance`). The venue's own minimum is `stops_level`; `min_stop_distance` is the SYSTEM's floor (`min_stop_set_by` names its rule, e.g. 0.5×ATR). `min_position_risk`: `fits_now` 1/0, `equity_for_min_lot`.
- `memory` holds the notes you wrote for yourself on the previous cycle for this pair (`notes`), with their time and your decision then.
- `management` rules you declare with a trade are executed by the system on the live position, in the execution instrument after translating your prices by the basis: `move_sl_to_breakeven` (the stop goes to the fill plus a spread buffer, never closer than the venue's stops level), `partial_close` (`params.fraction` of the trade's open volume, whole legs nearest target first; at the minimum lot it closes everything when the fraction is ≥ 0.5 and is skipped otherwise), `trail_atr` (`params.atr_mult` × ATR of the decision timeframe behind the price, moved once per closed decision candle), `trail_structure` (behind the last confirmed swing of the decision timeframe), `close_all`; triggers `tp_hit` (value = TP index; with a single position a nearer target becomes `price_reached` at that target, see rule 15), `price_reached` (a price), `r_multiple` (R from the original stop), `minutes_elapsed` (since the fill — a time stop together with `close_all`), `candle_close` (a decision-timeframe close beyond a price). The system only ever tightens a stop.
- `position_actions` act on live trades on a later cycle (see rule 13). `target.decision` is the 8-character `decision` id of an entry in `account.open_positions` or `account.pending_orders`; prices are in the analysis instrument's prices (the system translates them — never copy a price from the holdings without subtracting the basis); `close.fraction` is a share of the trade's open volume, taken from whole legs nearest target first (at the minimum lot: below 0.5 refused, 0.5 or more closes it); `modify_tp.leg` is the TP number of your original decision when several legs are open. Every action is checked (own trade, fresh quote, market open, a stop only tighter and at least the stops level plus spread from the price) and its result comes back to you as an `event:` trigger on the next cycle.
- The cycle's trigger names why you were called: a setup (`15m BOS …`, `price inside … order_block …`), `review condition: …` (one of your `next_review` conditions was met), `next_review time reached …` (only honoured when something changed since your last call), `event: …` (a fill, a take-profit or stop hit, a closed position, a settled outcome, an applied or refused `position_actions` entry), or `idle: …` (no review for a while).
- Charts, when attached, are listed in the cycle message (`Attached: …`): one image per timeframe of the last closed candles in the analysis instrument's prices, with the reference levels, zones, liquidity, structure events, EMA 20/50 and your live trades (entry, stop, targets) overlaid; volume panels of MT5-sourced pairs are tick volume.
- `history[].rejected_by` says who stopped a trade idea: `gate` (the system's risk rules — `gate_reason` lists every failed check in the execution instrument's prices), `broker` or `system` (`reject_reason`).
- `performance` is your record on this pair over the last days: cycles, trade ideas, how many the risk gate rejected, were not placed for other reasons, expired or were executed, and the virtual outcome of every trade idea measured on real prices (TP1 first, stop first, not triggered).
